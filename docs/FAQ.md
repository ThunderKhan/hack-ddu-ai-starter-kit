# Frequently asked questions

**Do I need a GPU?** No. Both prepared starters run on CPU using tiny datasets.

**Do I need to pay for AI APIs?** No. The prepared starters use scikit-learn and local sample data. Google Colab may have account and usage limits.

**Can I work solo?** Yes. Groups of 2–4 are easier to manage for a short hack sprint.

**Can I use another framework?** Yes, for Open Innovation, subject to event rules and licenses.

**Can I just submit the starter notebook?** It is fine as a tutorial, but **not** as an original competitive entry. Extend, evaluate and explain it.

**Can we train on real student messages?** No. Use fictional messages or properly authorized, non-identifiable data with appropriate permissions.

**Does a GitHub PR to Track B count for Track A?** No. Track B is first-contribution learning; Track A requires a separate team AI project and official submission where required.

**Does passing local judging mean an official MLH award?** Not necessarily. Official challenge eligibility and winner selection follow their own organizer instructions.

**Can we use generative AI to help code?** You must understand and verify what you submit. Follow organizer and event-specific AI-use policies.
