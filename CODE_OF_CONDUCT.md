# Community code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to learn and contribute regardless of experience level. Be respectful and inclusive; help others solve problems without ridicule or harassment.

- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Obtain consent before photographing someone, publishing identifying information or using their data.
- Do not share credentials, private messages or others' work without permission.
- Credit teammates and respect open-source licenses.
- Report in-person safety or conduct concerns privately to event organizers. Do not publish sensitive reports in GitHub issues.

Organizers may moderate content, remove inappropriate contributions and refer serious concerns to the university's appropriate channels.
